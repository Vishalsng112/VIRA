# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in VIRA a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

We act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and
healthy community.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the users who trust VIRA with
  private data.
- Do not publish others’ private information without permission.
- Do not use the project spaces for spam, harassment or bad-faith disruption.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others’ private information, credentials or device data.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
chats and events, and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that violate this code. Repeated or serious violations may result
in a temporary or permanent ban.

Report incidents privately to the maintainer through the GitHub profile listed
on the repository. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with respect
for the reporter’s privacy.
